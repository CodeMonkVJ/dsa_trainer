# 0/1 Knapsack

Implement `maximumValue(items, capacity)` for items represented by `{weight, value}`.

Each item may be selected at most once. Return the maximum value whose total weight is at most `capacity`.

Requirements:

- Handle zero capacity and items that do not fit.
- Do not reuse an item accidentally.
- Target complexity: `O(number of items * capacity)` time and `O(capacity)` space.
