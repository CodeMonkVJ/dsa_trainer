# Median of Two Sorted Arrays

Given two sorted arrays, return the median of all their values. The arrays may have different lengths, either may be empty, and they are not necessarily the same size. The combined input is guaranteed to contain at least one value.

Aim for `O(log(min(n, m)))` time and `O(1)` extra space. Return an exact integer median when the combined length is odd, or the average of the two middle values otherwise.

Implement `findMedianSortedArrays` in `starter.cpp`.
